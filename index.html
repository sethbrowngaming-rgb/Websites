I'm an indie game developer publishing under the studio name O2cool168 on Google Play, with a soft spot for the genre where a cold environment and a single warm light source do most of the storytelling — survival and horror. My first shipped title, COLD, started life as a Scratch project before I rebuilt it in Godot 4 and took it through Google Play's full publishing pipeline: AdMob, a privacy policy, and the Data Safety declarations most solo devs dread.

Alongside games, I build the platforms and infrastructure around them — Zebonastic is a Next.js site I write game-dev articles and build template tooling for, hosted straight off GitHub Pages. I'm currently studying Computer Science, starting at Snow College this fall before transferring to the University of Utah to finish my bachelor's degree.

My games are scattered across Google Play, itch.io, and Scratch — Scratch is where most of these prototypes actually started — and a Steam release is next on the roadmap.
